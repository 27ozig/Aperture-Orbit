Field Handbook & Guide
Aperture // Orbit Field Reference
1. Dedicated Camera Rig Setup (DSLR / Mirrorless)
• Manual Focus Pinpoint: Switch lens to MF. Set rear LCD to Live View, find a bright anchor star, digitally zoom to 10x, and turn the focus ring until the star is as small and crisp as possible.
• Lens Aberrations: Shoot wide open (f/1.4 - f/2.8). If coma distortion occurs in the corners, step down 1/3 stop.
• Noise Reduction: Turn off Long Exposure Noise Reduction in-camera so you can shoot consecutive frames without delay for stacking.
2. Companion Sky Apps (Pointing & Alignment)
• Android: Sky Map (open-source real-time celestial viewer)
• iOS Alternative: Stellarium Mobile or Night Sky for azimuth/zenith mapping.
3. Calendar Export (Long-Press)
Press and hold any starred card for 0.6 seconds to directly export the configured .ics alert to your calendar.
